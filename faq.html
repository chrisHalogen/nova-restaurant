Bookings

How do I book a table?
Send a request on the book a table page. We confirm on WhatsApp.
How long do you hold my table?
We hold your table for 15 minutes past the booking time.
Can I book for a large group?
Groups of eleven or more are hosted in the private room. See private dining.

Dining and menu

Do you show prices online?
Yes. Every price on the menu is in naira.
Do prices include VAT and service?
Prices include VAT. A 10 percent service charge is added to the bill.
Can you cater for allergies?
Tell your server before you order and the kitchen will guide you through the menu.

Bar and alcohol

Is there an age limit?
Guests must be 18 or over to order alcohol.
Do you have drinks without alcohol?
Yes, a full list of mocktails and soft drinks.
How late is the bar open?
Until 01:00 on Friday and Saturday, and until close on other nights.

Visiting

Where exactly are you?
RP Nexus Centre, Jakande, Lekki, Lagos. See visit.
Is there parking?
Yes, parking is available on site.
Is there a dress code?
Smart casual. No sportswear after 7pm.
Is the restaurant accessible?
There is step free access from the main entrance.

Online ordering

Can I order online?
Not yet. Online ordering for pickup and dine-in is coming soon, and delivery will follow. See order online.
Can I order by WhatsApp?
Message us and the team will help where they can.
