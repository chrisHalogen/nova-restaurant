Our story

A refined celebration of culture, connection and contemporary elegance.

NOVA opened at RP Nexus Centre to give Lekki a room that feels as considered as the food. The kitchen cooks the dishes people grew up with, plated with precision and finished over wood and charcoal.

The name is a promise: elevate every moment. A weekday lunch, a first date, a table of twelve celebrating a new job. The room is built to carry all of it, from the first pour at the bar to the last plate of the night.

The room

Warm materials, low light

Fluted wood panelling, brushed gold discs, a marble feature wall and planting through the room. The art on the walls is chosen to sit with those materials rather than compete with them.

The room

Fluted wood panelling, deep green velvet, brushed gold, marble floors and planting through the room. Forty seats, a bar counter, and a private room behind the arches.
