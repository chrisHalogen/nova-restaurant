Who we are

NOVA Restaurant operates at RP Nexus Centre, Jakande, Lekki, Lagos, and is part of RP Nexus. We are the data controller for the information described in this policy.

What we collect

We collect only what you give us through this website.

  • Table booking requests: name, phone number, date, time, party size, seating preference, occasion and any notes you add.
  • Private dining enquiries: the same details plus guest numbers and budget range.
  • Ordering notifications: your phone number, if you ask to be told when online ordering opens.
  • Website measurement: anonymous usage data, only after you accept cookies.

Why we use it

To confirm and manage your booking or enquiry, to answer your questions, and, where you have opted in, to send occasional news about menus and events. We do not sell your data.

Who sees it

Our restaurant and events team, and the service providers that host this website and deliver our messages. We share nothing else unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Booking and enquiry records are kept for as long as we need them to run the restaurant and meet our legal obligations, then deleted.

Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 you may ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete it, or withdraw consent for marketing at any time. Contact us and we will respond.

Cookies

See the cookie policy. You can change your choice at any time with the manage cookies link in the footer.
