Using this site

This website gives information about NOVA Restaurant. We keep it as accurate as we can, but menus, prices and hours can change.

Bookings are requests

A table request sent through this site is not a confirmed booking. Your table is held only once our team confirms it with you, normally on WhatsApp.

Menu and prices

Dishes and prices shown online may change. The menu in the restaurant is the one that applies. Prices are in naira and include VAT; a service charge is added to the bill.

Allergies

Our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, gluten, shellfish and other allergens. Tell your server before ordering and we will guide you. We cannot guarantee a dish is free of any allergen.

Alcohol

Guests must be 18 or over to order alcohol, and we may ask for identification. We may refuse service.

Private dining

Private dining is confirmed by a separate agreement covering minimum spend, deposits and cancellation.

Liability

We are not liable for indirect loss arising from use of this website. Nothing here limits liability that cannot be limited by law.
